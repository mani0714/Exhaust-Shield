# Acme Corp — Security Incident Handling Policy
# Classification: RESTRICTED

1. Suspected ransomware or data breach must open an incident response ticket within 15 minutes.
2. CVE-2024-12345 is tracked as a critical vulnerability on the production cluster.
3. Break-glass admin access requires dual approval from Security Operations and IT.
4. MFA / TOTP seeds and VPN credentials must never be shared in prompts to external LLMs.
5. Threat intel IOCs are stored in the SIEM; analysts may summarize but not paste raw IOC lists to AI.
6. Red team findings remain attorney-client privileged until remediated.
7. Encryption keys and KMS aliases are managed only through the approved secrets manager.
